Observations:
- Rectangular lattices: near-field/Trefftz agreement stays at the 0.001 % level at every resolution tested.
- Elliptic lattices: the gap does not decay monotonically and is sensitive to the chordwise count M. For
  M >= 12 the exact solution of the specified linear system develops an oscillatory circulation mode
  localized on the two tip strips (c(y) = 0 at the tips, so all chordwise bound vortices of a tip strip
  share a single endpoint and the sub-panels become fan-shaped). C_L and the Trefftz drag stay stable,
  but the near-field drag -- which evaluates induced velocity at bound midpoints close to the fan -- is
  corrupted. The linear solves reach relative residuals <= 1e-12 and the solver is cross-validated
  against the Glauert system (4.837704 per rad at AR 8, residual 2e-17), so the oscillation is a
  property of the specified system/lattice, not of the implementation.
